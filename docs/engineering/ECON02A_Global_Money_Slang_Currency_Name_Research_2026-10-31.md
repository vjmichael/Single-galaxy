# ECON-02A — World Money-Slang Research and Optional Single Galaxy Currency Naming

**2026-10-31 · naming research, not implemented money or a claim of exclusivity in any language.**

These are **examples of informal and colloquial money language**, not an exhaustive list and not necessarily the most widespread usage in a country. Some are ordinary local words for money, not slang; many are shared across borders; some denote a specific denomination rather than money generally. Preserve language and region differences when borrowing cultural words.

## Naming decision: propose, do not decree

The original colony has **finite people and no pre-established institutions**, so a galaxy-wide official currency cannot automatically exist on day one. The first settlement could exchange physical goods directly, keep reciprocal obligations/food-ration records, and later agree on a local fungible unit.

**Provisional colloquial name: `chits`.** It evokes labor vouchers and local credit without inventing a universal empire or mint. An administrative label might remain `settlement credit` or `ledger unit` until an actual organization issues and accepts it. A chit should represent a **verifiable claim** or ledger balance, never generate free goods. Other culturally varied name candidates: `guita`, `lana`, `grana`, `kohle`, `mangır`, `chavos`, or a completely invented word. No single choice has been approved by the player or implemented in gameplay.

**Economic engineering priority:** build physical goods, labor, trusted ownership, exchange and obligation ledgers before establishing who can issue currency, what backs it, whether colonies accept one another's notes, and how inflation/default/competition work. Market value is not a hard-coded static sell price.
