# WO5 — Physical transport, logistics, and resource-demand integration
**Opened 2026-11-07**, owner-directed Stage 5. Branch `industry/wo5-physical-logistics-20261107` stacks on WO4 draft PR #91; WO4 stacks on WO3 #89.

## Binding architecture and scope
- The game begins with finite physically constructed ships, planetary shuttles, storage, fuel and original NPC pilots. No free carrier, automatically completed shipment, arbitrary planetary goods, transport speed, trade credit, wage or material creation.
- `Simulation` and `PersistentWorld` remain the only stock, NPC and ship authority. Original `UniversalTransport`, `IntercolonyFreightLeg`, `LocalDeliveryOrder`, `ThirdPartyRegistry` and `RegionalHostCommands` already exist; WO5 must extend/compose rather than replace them.
- WO4 `CarrierRequest` and `ProposedMaterialFreight` are **read-only unbooked suggestions**, never arrival receipts or owner approvals. Every operation must be owner-authenticated by the trusted host and recheck the current original world, not a copied demand snapshot.
- The actual original local pickup supports **Water, Food, Nutrients**. The original two-leg sealed intercolony freight supports these same three, NOT oxygen or arbitrary manufactured objects. The separate original planetary shuttle supports Water, Food, Nutrients, Oxygen for one local trip. Finished panel/frame cargo has its own original `ThirdPartyRegistry` path and requires real constructed workshops, distinct owner approvals and warehouse custody.
- WO3-F remains open due pre-runner CI infrastructure; WO4-F remains open until genuine native test logs. Neither blocks Stage 5 development. Native Windows 3D installed-game acceptance stays deferred until genuine 3D assets.

## WO5-A physical integration committed
- New header `include/galaxy/WO5PhysicalResupply.hpp` composes the real `economy04::CarrierRequest` with original `Simulation::load_planetary_shuttle` and `PersistentWorld::load_planetary_shuttle`; demand is regenerated against the **current world hash/tick**, owner and physical original site/shuttle, and a throwaway original Simulation preflights exact native cargo/berth/capacity. Surface resource shortages cannot be converted into fictional deliverable stock.
- Only a built, owner-controlled, orbital-docked shuttle with a named pilot, empty family/passenger manifest and enough physically held water propellant can accept the proposed leg. Requested kg must fit the 1-tonne original shared hold and the owner's actual unreserved supply buffer; the existing world is not touched by planning.
- Trusted explicit owner approval invokes exactly **one** existing SQLite-atomic `PersistentWorld::load_planetary_shuttle`. After this, the cargo is on the original shuttle, not at the site. Launch and the actual three-tick flight remain original `PersistentWorld::launch_planetary_shuttle` plus `steps`/native shuttle receipts.
- `commit_approved_landed_unload` requires original landed phase and exact real descent receipt, current original owner/craft/site, valid native held kg, and revalidates the original unload on a scratch Simulation. Only `PersistentWorld::unload_planetary_shuttle` can credit destination stock. No second shipment table, fulfillment flag or invented person.
- The **existing true WO3 planetary utility fixture** now runs the entire WO5-A route in an **independent recovered SQLite copy**, with a real constructed site/vehicle, real named pilot, physical water fuel, real food shortage, owner consent, hostile/stale/oversize refusals, injected SQLite `AfterSnapshotWrite` rollback, original descent receipt, withheld-before-landing and duplicate-unload refusal, conserved matter and another recovered copy proving physical destination stock. This source test is registered as `galaxy_wo3_planetary_utility` and **not yet natively executed at new WO5 head**.

## WO5-B actual original booking committed

- `book_approved_intercolony_pickup` checks trusted owner approval and exact still-current world hash/tick, then performs the existing **single-transaction** `PersistentWorld::post_intercolony_work_order`.
