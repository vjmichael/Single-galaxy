## Separating game rules from research

The real-world sources justify **which** stages and capabilities are needed. Candidate gram quantities, item masses, volumes, machine energy/tick costs and food serving amounts are **tentative Single-Galaxy balancing**. In particular raw ore refinement, crops and nutrients, clean-room electronics, cement calcination, process gas/water sinks, and semiconductor/medical production cannot become live commands without corresponding built facilities, stocked inputs, worker qualification and verified environmental sources. Existing source gameplay mass conservation is never relaxed. Some detailed trade/technical research and balancing will necessarily occur when those capabilities are activated in their later approved work orders.

Data-only lifecycle/spoilage and needs **do not** mutate the NPC welfare/population system. No separate industrial persistence store or currency was introduced.
