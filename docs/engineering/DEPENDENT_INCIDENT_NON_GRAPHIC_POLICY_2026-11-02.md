# Single-Galaxy — Dependent incident and presentation safety policy

**Confirmed user design decision: 2026-11-02.**

## Mandatory content boundary

**Never depict or visualize children being harmed or dying.** No child injury/death animations, bodies, kill cams, harm closeups, cinematic reenactments, combat effects, or promotional visuals showing such events. Children are not directly targetable combat entities.

An authoritative simulation may eventually need non-graphic, internally recorded dependent statuses to preserve family and population continuity during a large disaster. A server-level fate or missing-person record is not a rendered scene and must not be presented graphically. Keep public notification abstract and restrained, and provide options to filter sensitive written notices when a client UI is built.

## Engineering constraints

- Preserve distinctions between aboard, safe, rescued, unaccounted for, and confirmed deceased. Never infer death merely from absence, a crash, or a delayed journey.
- Every incident must be explicitly authorized, recorded, and independently recoverable. Do not invent survivors or casualties to force conservation.
- Continue refusing fatal incidents with active dependent ferry berths until durable guardian/custody, family history, regional population, rescue and outcome reconciliation exist.
- Keep NPC identities, parentage and historical relationships in the canonical ledger; no child cloning or disappearing dependents.
- Fire/police/full EMS institutions remain a separately deferred development track.

**Status:** Policy documented and current focused-kernel fatal-dependent refusal preserved. This does NOT claim the full GUI, cinematic or public-service systems exist or already enforce the policy.
