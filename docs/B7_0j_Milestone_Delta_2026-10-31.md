# B7.0j design delta — keep historical specs intact

The 2026-10-30 `SingleGalaxy_Consolidated_Feature_Spec_2026-10-30.md` predates B7.0c–j and remains the *design north star*, not a claim of implementation. See per-milestone engineering reports for verified executable features.

New executable B7.0j slice: built community halls, real on-site food/water supply and waste mass, bounded resident attendants, dependable social-access continuity, site-specific resilience reason, and compatibility-preserving C++/local-relay/UI contracts. Services do not become a universal happiness bonus or certify clinical health, water renewal, NPC growth or server-offline play. Future design for families, institutions and continuously simulated 3D space is **not** implemented in this slice.
