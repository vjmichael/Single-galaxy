# PLAY-11 — Ship Fitting and Exploration Architecture
**2026-11-04 · Single Galaxy authoritative design decision · Implementation branch `integration/play11-ship-fitting-exploration-20261104`**

## Design source and priority
These are player-selected design constraints, not optional decoration. The initial PLAY-11 code delivers only the narrow fitted-exploration-scanner and safety-sensor vertical slice described below. Later tasks should extend this one fitting schema instead of letting every ship carry unlimited equipment.

## Four finite ship-module categories

| Class | What it changes | Player examples | Constraints |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hardpoint** | Active equipment that causes **world interactions** | exploration scanner, radar array, LiDAR mapper, mining laser, weapons, tractor beam, probe launcher, drone operations module, biological/life detector | Fits in real hardpoint socket; invoked via native authorized command; requires operational ship resources |
| **Support** | Passive aids to fitted hardpoints | tracking enhancer, range extender, multi-target lock, scan-resolution enhancer, sensor noise reduction | Occupies support socket; cannot generate independent actions; applies bounded bonuses only to compatible active module |
| **Subsystem** | Internal ship equipment | power plant augmentation, processing-speed booster, data analyzer, shield booster, communications uplink, hacking subsystem | Occupies subsystem socket; power/CPU/heat/cooling resource budgets in future; never a license to bypass sensor or contact privacy |
| **Rigging** | Physical ship properties | armor/hull reinforcement, additional cargo volume, cloaking modifications, propulsion, shield-envelope shaping | Occupies rig socket; modifies actual hull physics and persists; retrofit costs, tradeoffs and removal penalties later |

**PLAY-11 introductory hull rack capacities:** 2 hardpoints, 2 support, 2 subsystem, 1 rig. These are *introductory values*, **not** fixed capacities for every future hull. Physical rack state is serialized into native **v64**. Module ID 0 = empty, 1 = active local exploration scanner (valid only in a hardpoint). Support, subsystem and rig IDs are reserved as empty until real modules have validated definitions, production recipes and effects. A scanner cannot occupy two slots at once. Future hull classes will have distinct rack capacities, power, CPU, mass, thermal and calibration budgets, ship size and attachment geometry.

### PLAY-11 implemented starter scanner
- A **newly generated** player colony ship has one real pre-mounted exploration scanner in hardpoint #1, represented as **2 tonnes of embodied construction-module-equivalent mass**. It is a *starting physical asset*, not loot spawned on scan.
- Scans are **not available** when the ship lacks the fitted module. Scanner presence is checked **inside native `SurveyLocal`**, not in browser flags.
- The player can remove a scanner while stopped, returning exactly one physical construction module to onboard inventory; refitting consumes it. This is an introductory material-accounting stand-in for eventual dedicated equipment fabrication, not the final scanner crafting recipe. Total world material remains conserved, including mounted equipment and carrier wrecks.
- Old saved ships do **not** automatically receive free scanners. They must produce an onboard module via existing refinement/fabrication and fit it using native authorized commands.
- A 50,000 mm **local exploration survey** records real currently present nearby asteroids and preserves first-observation facts in the v63 discovery ledger.
- The ship also has a **separate, always-on close-proximity sensor**, providing a 5,000 mm native nearest-ship contact advisory. This is **situational awareness**, not a physics collision-detection or collision-response system. Dedicated collision handling remains a separate milestone.
- This is intentionally the first step toward multiple hardpoints, not a general replacement for combat, mining, or other active actions yet.

## Dedicated exploration equipment roadmap (not implemented in PLAY-11)
**Long-range scanners:** broad-field astronomy, long-range radar or passive signal analysis; distance reduces identification confidence; signal propagation and obstacle occlusion eventually matter. Different scanners can discover objects without identifying exact contents. No free map-wide reveal.

**Probe launcher and recoverable probes:** finite deployable physical units with communication windows and battery/fuel; selectable sensor payloads; probes launch from a compatible fitted hardpoint and are tracked as world entities with ownership and custody.

**Drones:** persistent drone bays, control bandwidth, communications dependency, batteries, mass, maximum range and risk of loss. Specialized geological drill drones interact with asteroids, moons and planets to collect samples; surface, orbit, and subsurface sample provenance must be explicit. Do not let “scan” secretly drill or produce resources.

**Instrument options:** LiDAR for surface geometry and ranging; radar for large object/range detection; optical/infrared spectroscopy for material signatures; sonar only where a **physical sound-propagating medium** exists (liquids, gas/atmosphere or a solid substrate), **not** as vacuum-space ship sonar. Material analyzers for resource-specific chemistry/isotopes; life-sign scanners capable of identifying humans and ordinary biology (no intelligent alien civilizations currently exist by design).

**Exploration infrastructure:** player-created sensor beacons, survey satellites, orbital relays, communications datalink hubs and mapping repositories; their power, maintenance, bandwidth, construction mass and vulnerability are real. Sharing/discovery licensing/credits and multi-user trust must be future explicit designs.

## Space environments and navigation roadmap (not implemented in PLAY-11)
- **Comms deadzones:** attenuation, disruptions, delayed data transmission, lost sensor uplinks; never delete durable onboard observations arbitrarily. Risk and recovery possible.
- **Dense regions:** spatial drag/collision hazard and speed restrictions grounded in actual region physics; apply consistently to ships and projectiles.
- **Solar radiation/activity:** heat, electronics degradation, shield/power efficiency, sensor interference, crew health risk with protective equipment and warnings.
- **Cosmic winds / useful flow fields:** bounded direction-dependent acceleration; physically coherent hazards and aids.
- **Gas, ice, rocks, fields and irregular regions:** surveyable physical content and differing resource extraction procedures, not reskinned asteroids.
- **Fold space / wormholes:** region-to-region shortcuts, with stability, navigation conditions, and mass-dependent transit/cooldown potentially distinct. No faster-than-light ordinary inertial motion required.
- **Terminology:** **folding** is the official instantaneous spatial-distance-collapse transition; player colloquialism “jump” and “jump gate” remains acceptable but UI/design canonical names are **Fold** and **Fold Gate**. Ship size can affect *fold preparation, transition or recovery duration*; do not imply smaller vessels occupy less distance once folded. Existing historical gate code should be renamed carefully with persistent format compatibility.
- **Warp:** very-high-speed but **sublight** ship travel through physical space, separate from instantaneous folding. Ships move along a real trajectory; they never pass through solid bodies. A hyperlane is an *acceleration booster*, not a teleportation jump, an enclosed tunnel, or a bidirectional paired-gate link.
- **Hyperlane mechanics (player correction, Nov 4, 2026):** each player-built hyperlane installation has exactly **one entry direction and one exit direction**. A ship flies into its entry and is propelled forward at greatly increased speed along a defined, checked corridor toward the destination. The booster imparts velocity/warp assistance, not a distance-skipping Fold. Reverse travel requires a **separately constructed, independently powered reverse-direction hyperlane**; a single lane never works both ways. The entry/exit facing, permitted trajectories, speed/acceleration profile, compatible ship mass/size classes, power or fuel budget, cooldown/traffic handling, warning and emergency exit behavior must be represented by native simulation state, not UI-only effects. Player control, maneuvering and deceleration requirements must be modeled at departure/arrival. Intersections and back-to-back boosts form a directed network.
- **Size classes:** **small**, **medium**, and **large** hyperlanes are all permitted. This replaces the previously universal 0.5 AU minimum spacing: small installations may boost over local/sub-AU distances, medium installations serve larger regional corridors, and large installations serve long interplanetary/interstellar corridors. **Exact minimum/maximum length, achievable speed, power, construction cost, ship size/mass restrictions, and acceleration are deliberately not set yet** and require simulation balancing. The earlier **0.5 AU idea remains a candidate for large installations only**, not a rule for smaller tiers. Do not silently apply a fixed distance to all hyperlane sizes.
- **Voluntary warp disengagement and emergency breakaway (player addition, Nov 4, 2026):** **all ships, player-controlled and NPC-operated, may request to exit normal warp or a hyperlane-assisted warp corridor before its scheduled endpoint.** A hyperlane provides a one-way entry boost, not an unbreakable conveyor that captures a ship until arrival. Exit requests are native, deterministic ship-state transitions with a finite deceleration/warp-drop sequence; the ship emerges at its *actual intermediate physical position* in the same coordinate frame, not teleported to its destination and not automatically stopped. Preserve residual velocity and require appropriate braking/flight control. Real obstacles and unsafe exit geometry must matter; the system should prevent impossible penetrations and provide a physically coherent emergency behavior rather than disallowing all manual exits. Both AI and humans use the same authority, eligibility and movement constraints. A stranded or damaged ship may have degraded deceleration and face danger. Navigation displays need an explicit **Exit Warp / Breakaway** control, remaining travel distance, speed/velocity, current route direction, and arrival/drop-out risk; automated NPC route planners need sensible default breakaway decisions. Exiting a one-way hyperlane does **not** create a reverse-direction lane, cancel past motion, or refund consumed boost energy. Rejoining after an exit requires reaching an authorized entry/re-entry point and meeting its operating rules. The precise dropout envelope, deceleration, hazards, combat interception and emergency failure mechanics require implementation and tuning later; do not implement as a client-side coordinate edit.
- **Hyperlanes are explorable one-way highways (player clarification, Nov 4, 2026):** their primary value is enabling long routes *through* space, not skipping the space between endpoints. A deep-space probe can locate a promising world; the player can plan and physically construct a **directed hyperlane corridor** from their colony toward it. They can ride the route to transport people/materials, **exit deliberately anywhere along a safe stretch** to investigate intermediate space, then travel onward using ordinary flight or an appropriately constructed/reachable hyperlane entry. This makes the territory between distant destinations a persistent explorable environment containing discoveries, resources, hazards and later outposts. There is **no requirement to travel to the terminal endpoint** or to have known every intermediate discovery before construction, but engineering must first survey the corridor well enough to prove a safe path.
- **On-ramps, off-ramps, and continuing a journey:** a hyperlane consists of a directional entry/acceleration point and a physical route/corridor that ships may voluntarily leave; safe drop-out at arbitrary reachable mid-route positions is an ordinary supported action, **not automatically classified as an emergency**. An **off-ramp** is a controlled exit maneuver that conserves actual position and velocity; a **breakaway** is the exceptional faster/riskier disengagement under danger or changed circumstances. Safe re-entry/continuation is a separate issue: do **not** assume an exited ship can instantly regain hyperlane boost at any arbitrary mid-lane coordinate. An access node/booster, navigation alignment procedure, or sufficient self-propulsion must provide a physical way to accelerate back onto the route. Players may construct smaller local feeders/on-ramps, turnoffs and branch links as resource-backed infrastructure, without turning a one-way lane into a two-way one.
- **Discovery-to-infrastructure loop:** deep-space probe detects candidate planet → player verifies habitability/resources and plans a collision-free directional highway → nearby available NPC crews haul finite supplies and construct it → player and NPC ships use the corridor → explorers drop out en route and survey nearby sectors → newly discovered deposits/sites can motivate additional off-ramps, small feeder lanes, beacons, logistics hubs and eventually settlements. Preserve original discovery provenance, ownership, construction effort and the costs of off-route travel; do not reveal all intermediate sites merely because a highway passes through them.
- **Network and user interface:** map routes as **directed edges** with size class, source/terminal, active direction, available entrances/branch nodes, known destinations, distance-to-exit, current boost/warp state and safe drop-out advisories. The player can select an end destination or a surveyed intermediate waypoint, **Exit Warp** at a selected safe point or initiate an emergency breakaway; NPC-operated ships must obey identical off-ramp/physics rules. Planned branches require corridor clearance and construction, not a magic UI splice.
- **Placement and physical safety:** the player can lay out a directed chain or network of proposed hyperlanes from the galaxy macro map. A constructed link must pass a swept-path obstacle test with margins against stars, planets, moons, asteroids, built infrastructure, moving hazards and unsafe entry/exit geometry. No sun, moon or planet clipping; no EVE-style travel through opaque celestial bodies. Any link whose corridor is obstructed is refused, rerouted, or remains in planning state until resolved. For construction, nearby tasked NPC labor and actual hauled materials/power infrastructure are required; marking placements on the macro map does not instantly create physical hardware.
- **Scale constraints:** current `kWorldBoundMm=1,000,000,000` supports only small local-world coordinates; true sub-AU, AU and light-year travel needs a cosmic coordinate/partition model and physically meaningful time-scale changes. Do not claim large space-wide hyperlanes work under the current coordinate bound. Short local experimental boosters can be prototyped separately when native swept-corridor checks and physics are implemented.
- **NPC travel control:** NPC-operated freighters, escorts, explorers, and other ships may proactively leave warp or abandon a hyperlane route for hazards, revised orders, obstruction, rescue or encounters. Their steering follows the same physics and safety checks as the player ship; a route change never grants NPC-only teleportation.
- **NPC logistics:** laying out a hyperlane is a proposed construction project, not an instant deployment. Crews, materials, fuel, workforce, safe routes and execution history are committed in the existing authoritative logistics/world model.

## Mandatory future engineering gates
1. Generalized module registry and ship-class fitting plans: hardpoint/support/subsystem/rigging socket compatibility, power/compute/heat/mass budget and slot-fit transactions, cargo custody, hull invariants, snapshot recovery, manufacturing and field servicing.
2. Scan attenuation and dedicated sensor modality / physical probe/drone lifecycles with strict source provenance; multi-stage discovery (detect → localize → characterize → verify).
3. Proximity collision avoidance and actual ship–body collision physics in native simulation, independent of scanning.
4. Real celestial bodies, gas/ice/resources, environmental physics and safe world-state effects, with no magic resource creation.
5. Introduce a native, **one-way** small/medium/large highway-style hyperlane accelerator with **ordinary safe intermediate off-ramps, distinct emergency breakaways, physical re-entry/on-ramps and player/NPC waypoint exits**, separately constructed reverse-route access, power/traffic/velocity custody, swept-path obstacle checks and NPC-dependent fabrication. Prototype only truly local small boosters under existing coordinates; expand cosmic coordinate/scale support before AU/light-year corridors. Keep instantaneous Fold Gate transitions independent of sublight warp acceleration.
6. Persistence/CI/native owner-auth acceptance on Windows and Linux, including save upgrades and older snapshots.
7. Redesign finite weapon ammunition together with the future comprehensive weapon, manufacturing and logistics system. PLAY-08's 12-shot training charge remains a temporary fixture, **not** the final ammunition design.
