B7.0f · Player-built habitation

A habitat costs 2 modules and shelters up to 8 people (including the outpost's farm workers). Transfer food, water and oxygen for new residents; nobody consumes supplies twice. Three consecutive properly provisioned periods count toward Livable, but do not certify independent growth.

B7.0g · Player-built solar utility

Build solar service at a player-owned outpost for 2 modules. Power is generated and stored locally; staffed farms and occupied habitats draw from it. Service equipment wears, can fail, and consumes a real manufactured module when repaired. A powered habitat alone does not certify self-sufficiency.

B7.0h · Local technicians and spare-part maintenance

Technicians must already be residents in the selected habitat. Deliver manufactured modules physically from the nearby colony ship before leaving. A standing repair order consumes one stored module when condition falls to 40% or below. No remote inventory transfers, resource spawning, or autonomous expansion.

B7.0j · Community hall and restorative social service

Construct a hall beside existing housing (2 manufactured modules). Assign actual housed residents as attendants, physically deliver food and water, and keep local solar power functioning. One attendant serves up to 4 people per needs cycle; each 4 visits consume an additional 1 kg of food and water, producing local waste. Three consecutive fully supplied cycles demonstrate access to restorative leisure—not clinical healthcare, universal happiness, or safe autonomous growth.

B7.0k-2 · Local water/oxygen reclamation

Build a reclamation unit next to housing and solar service, using 2 real manufactured modules. Each 20-tick needs period it can process up to 10 kg of wastewater and 10 kg of CO₂ from EACH site compartment: 10 wastewater → 9 clean water + 1 sludge; 10 CO₂ → 7 oxygen + 3 carbon-rich residue. All matter stays on site. A powered plant adds 1 unit per tick to demand. The rolling three-period assessment measures actual production against need, but finite feedstock and losses mean this is NOT proof of indefinite water renewal.

B7.0l · Mineral water purification and delivery

Mine water-bearing asteroid minerals such as serpentine or saponite, separate the water-bearing concentrate into raw water goods, and bring sealed 1,000 kg tank loads in a colony ship to this player-built waterworks. Each 20-tick period with local solar service the plant can purify 20 kg of raw feed into 18 kg potable water stored in the local habitat plus 2 kg retained brine. The finite mine and local delivery distance matter. Three successful periods measure replenishment, NOT indefinite water renewal or autonomous growth.
